Kala Anger Conversation Theory

Formula:

$$ E_{\\text{anger}}(t) = \\left( m_{\\text{words}} \\cdot c_{\\text{emotion}}^2 \\right) \\cdot \\left(1 - \\Phi(t) \\right) $$

Explanation:

This formula expresses how emotional energy during anger-driven conversations can be calculated. - $$m_{\\text{words}}$$ is the mental weight or intensity of the words used (scale of 0–10). - $$c_{\\text{emotion}}$$ is the emotional speed — how fast emotions are released (scale of 0–10). - $$\\Phi(t)$$ is the Kala Time Expansion function (value from 0 to 1), representing how much your time sense is expanded.

When $$\\Phi(t) = 1$$, time is expanded and anger energy becomes 0. When $$\\Phi(t) = 0$$, time is collapsed and anger is at full release.
