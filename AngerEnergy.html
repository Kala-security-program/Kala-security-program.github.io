Kala Anger Conversation Theory

1. Formula

$$E_{\\text{anger}}(t) = \\left( m_{\\text{words}} \\cdot c_{\\text{emotion}}^2 \\right) \\cdot \\left(1 - \\Phi(t)\\right)$$

2. Explanation

In real life, this formula measures how much emotional energy is released as anger over time.
- \( m_{\text{words}} \) is the "mass" or weight of words (how heavy or sharp they are).
- \( c_{\text{emotion}} \) is the emotional intensity of the conversation.
- \( \Phi(t) \) is Kala's Time Expansion Function, which increases with calmness and reduces anger.

As time passes and \( \Phi(t) \to 1 \), the anger energy drops to zero. If time is compressed (\( \Phi(t) \to 0 \)), anger is fully released.
