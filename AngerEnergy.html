⚛ Kala Anger Conversation Theory

Formula:
\\[ E_{\\text{anger}}(t) = \\left( m_{\\text{words}} \\cdot c_{\\text{emotion}}^2 \\right) \\cdot (1 - \\Phi(t)) \\]
Explanation:
This formula calculates the **emotional energy** released during an angry moment.

- \( m_{\text{words}} \): Number of intense words spoken (mental mass)
- \( c_{\text{emotion}} \): Emotional delivery speed (tone, intensity)
- \( \Phi(t) \): Kala Time Expansion — your mind's ability to slow down/react

The higher the emotion and lower the time expansion, the more energy is released.
