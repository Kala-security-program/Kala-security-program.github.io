🌌 Core Time Expansion Formula:

In Kala Theory, the behavior of time under gravity and oscillation is described by:

$$ \Phi(t) = \sqrt{1 - \frac{2GM}{rc^2}} \cdot \left(1 + \alpha \sin(\omega t)\right) $$

🧠 Explanation:

This formula combines two major phenomena:

  • Einstein's General Relativity: The first term, \( \sqrt{1 - \frac{2GM}{rc^2}} \), is the gravitational time dilation near a massive body.
  • Kala Time Oscillation: The second term \( 1 + \alpha \sin(\omega t) \) introduces time waves or oscillations over time, controlled by:
    • \( \alpha \): amplitude of oscillation
    • \( \omega \): frequency of oscillation
